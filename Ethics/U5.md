## Unit V – Companies: IT & ITES Industry, NASSCOM, STPI, Trends, Diversity

## 1. Introduction to IT and ITES Industry

**IT = Information Technology**

- Deals with software, hardware, networks, systems, data, and their use for processing and storing information.[[testbook](https://testbook.com/full-form/ites-full-form)]
    

**ITES = Information Technology Enabled Services**

- Services delivered using IT, e.g.:
    
    - Customer support / call centers
        
    - Back‑office operations
        
    - E‑banking, e‑health, e‑education
        
    - Digital marketing, online tutoring, telemedicine
        
- IT uses technology; ITES uses IT to deliver services to businesses and customers.[[scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/863967455/CSE332-Lecture-18-LPU-Official)]
    

**Types of IT companies**:[[scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/863967455/CSE332-Lecture-18-LPU-Official)]

|Aspect|Product‑based Companies|Services‑based Companies|
|---|---|---|
|What they sell|Their own software/hardware products|IT services/solutions to clients|
|Revenue model|Product sales, licenses, subscriptions|Project fees, hourly/contract rates|
|R&D focus|High R&D for own products|Adapting tech to client needs|
|Examples|Microsoft, Google, Zoho, Freshworks|TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL|
|Client relationship|Global customers, indirect|Direct client contracts|

**Key points for exams**:

- Product‑based = own product developed and sold globally.
    
- Services‑based = provide IT services (development, testing, consulting, support) to clients.

## 2. Introduction to NASSCOM

**NASSCOM** = **National Association of Software and Service Companies**.[[en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASSCOM)]

**Key facts (important for GK/time‑line questions)**:

- **Established**: **1 March 1988**.[[devex](https://www.devex.com/organizations/national-association-of-software-and-service-companies-nasscom-147875)]
    
- **Type**: Non‑government, non‑profit trade association and advocacy group.[[en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASSCOM)]
    
- **Role**: Apex body for India’s **IT‑BPM** (Information Technology – Business Process Management) industry.[[nasscom](https://nasscom.in/about-us)]
    
- **Industry size**: Represents India’s **~$283 billion** technology industry (as per recent reports).[[nasscom](https://nasscom.in/about-us)]
    
- **Members**: Over **3,000+** companies (about 90% of industry revenue).[[devex](https://www.devex.com/organizations/national-association-of-software-and-service-companies-nasscom-147875)]
    

**Main functions of NASSCOM**:[[en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASSCOM)]

- Policy advocacy with government for IT industry.
    
- Setting service quality standards.
    
- Promoting intellectual property rights in software and BPO.
    
- Talent development, skill programs, industry–academia collaboration.
    
- Supporting startups (e.g., 10,000 Startups initiative).
    
- Promoting Indian IT globally, building India as a trusted tech hub.
    

**ELICIT values**: Excellence, Learning, Impact, Collaboration, Inclusion, Trust.[[nasscom](https://nasscom.in/about-us)]

## 3. Overview of STPI (Software Technology Parks of India)

**STPI** = **Software Technology Parks of India**.[[indiascienceandtechnology.gov](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/organisations/ministry-and-departments/ministry-electronics-and-information-technology-meity-govt-india/software-technology-parks-india-stpi-uttar-pradesh)][[youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IogRLpp7ks)][[stpi](https://stpi.in/en/about-stpi)]

**Key timeline facts**:

- **Established**: **1991** (specifically **5 June 1991**) as an autonomous society.[[vajiramandravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/software-technology-parks-of-india-stpi/)][[youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IogRLpp7ks)][[indiascienceandtechnology.gov](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/organisations/ministry-and-departments/ministry-electronics-and-information-technology-meity-govt-india/software-technology-parks-india-stpi-uttar-pradesh)]
    
- **Under Ministry**: **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**.[[stpi](https://stpi.in/en/about-stpi)]
    
- **Purpose**: Promote software development and **software exports** from India.[[indiascienceandtechnology.gov](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/organisations/ministry-and-departments/ministry-electronics-and-information-technology-meity-govt-india/software-technology-parks-india-stpi-uttar-pradesh)]
    

**Main functions/services of STPI**:[[youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IogRLpp7ks)][[vajiramandravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/software-technology-parks-of-india-stpi/)]

- Implement **STP Scheme** and **EHTP Scheme** for IT/ITES exporters.
    
- Provide **plug‑and‑play office space** with IT infrastructure.
    
- Facilitate **duty‑free import** of capital goods, consumables for export.
    
- Single‑window clearance for customs and regulatory compliance.
    
- Support for **startups**:
    
    - Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoEs)
        
    - Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS)
        
- Incubation services, datacom services, VAPT, BPO schemes.
    
- Promote emerging tech: IoT, Blockchain, AI, ML, Computer Vision, Gaming, Animation.[[vajiramandravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/software-technology-parks-of-india-stpi/)][[youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IogRLpp7ks)][[stpi](https://stpi.in/en/about-stpi)]
    

**Growth**:

- Software exports from STPI‑registered companies grew from **₹52 crore in 1991** to over **₹7 lakh crore in 2023**.[[youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IogRLpp7ks)]
    
- From **3 STP centers in 1990s** to **63 centers today**.[[youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IogRLpp7ks)]

## 5. Case Study of an IT Industry (Product‑based & Services‑based)

You should know at least one **product‑based** and one **services‑based** case.

## Product‑based Example: Zoho

- **What**: SaaS product company (CRM, email, office suite, finance, etc.).
    
- **Model**: Sell own software products globally via subscription.
    
- **Key points**:
    
    - Based in Chennai, India.
        
    - Focus on privacy, no ads, long‑term product building.
        
    - Global customers, many SMEs.
        

## Services‑based Example: TCS (Tata Consultancy Services)

- **What**: IT services, consulting, system integration, testing, support.
    
- **Model**: Client‑based projects, contracts.
    
- **Key points**:
    
    - Part of Tata Group.
        
    - One of largest IT services companies globally.
        
    - Works with global enterprises across industries.
        

**Exam pattern**:

- Product‑based → own product, global sales.
    
- Services‑based → IT solutions for clients, project‑based revenue.

## 8. Diversity in the Workforce

**Diversity** = inclusion of people with different:

- Gender, caste, religion, nationality
    
- Age, disability
    
- Socio‑economic background, education
    

**Why important in IT**:[[facebook](https://www.facebook.com/GREATPLACETOWORKINSTITUTEINDIA/posts/diversity-in-the-workforce-brings-valuable-perspectives-attracts-top-talent-and-/642553537904509/)]

- Brings diverse perspectives → better innovation and problem‑solving.
    
- Attracts top talent from all backgrounds.
    
- Helps understand diverse customer bases.
    
- Improves company reputation and culture.
    

**Common initiatives**:

- Gender diversity programs (women in tech).
    
- Inclusion for disabled employees.
    
- Equal opportunity policies.
    
- Mentorship and leadership programs for underrepresented groups.
    

For exam:

- Diversity → variety of backgrounds.
    
- Benefits → innovation, talent, customer understanding, reputation.
