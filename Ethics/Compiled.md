## Unit I – Ethics & IT Ethics (Quick)

## Core Concepts

- **Ethics**: Moral principles guiding what is right/wrong in behavior and decisions.
    
- **Business ethics**: Applying ethics to business activities and policies.
    
- **Integrity**: Doing the right thing consistently, even when no one is watching.
    
- **Goal**: Build trust, reputation, long‑term success; reduce legal risk.
    

## Business Ethics – Key Points

- Benefits:
    
    - Trust of customers, employees, investors.
        
    - Long‑term profits and brand value.
        
    - Better talent attraction and retention.
        
- Tools to improve ethics:
    
    - Written **code of ethics / code of conduct**.
        
    - **Tone at the top** – leaders must act ethically.
        
    - **Ethics training** (usually annual).
        
    - **Whistle‑blower** channels.
        
    - Periodic **ethics audits**.
        

## Ethical Work Environment

- Clear policies: conflict of interest, gifts, harassment, discrimination, privacy.
    
- Transparent communication.
    
- Reward ethical behavior, not just results.
    
- Respect diversity and women empowerment.
    

## Ethics in IT

- Issues:
    
    - Privacy, security, piracy, plagiarism.
        
    - Misuse of social media, cyberbullying.
        
    - Digital divide.
        
- IT professionals:
    
    - Protect confidentiality, integrity, availability.
        
    - Avoid conflicts of interest.
        
    - Respect IP and licenses.

## Unit II – Intellectual Property (IP) – HIGHLY IMPORTANT FOR DURATIONS

## IP Types & Durations (India)

|IP Type|What it Protects|Duration / Term|Renewal? / After Term|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Patent**|New inventions (product/process)|**20 years** from filing date|No extension; lapses; then public domain|
|**Copyright**|Original expression (books, music, films, software, etc.)|**Life of author + 60 years** (most works) / **60 years** from publication for films, govt, etc.|No renewal needed|
|**Trademark**|Brand name, logo, symbol for goods/services|**10 years** initial term from registration|Renewable every **10 years**, indefinitely|
|**Design**|Aesthetic shape/appearance of product|**10 years** + extension **5 years** = max **15 years**|Only once (10+5)|

## Trademarks – Types / Marks

- **Brand name** – word(s) as mark.
    
- **Logo / symbol** – graphic sign.
    
- **Service mark** – for services (IT services, airlines, etc.).
    
- **Certification mark** – certifies quality/origin (owned by certifying body).
    
- **Collective mark** – used by association members.
    
- **Well‑known mark** – famous mark with extra protection.
    

## Why Patent Information Matters for Business

- Avoids duplicate R&D.
    
- Shows technology trends and competitor activity.
    
- Helps identify white spaces (new opportunities).
    
- Basis for licensing, partnerships, strategy.

## Unit III – Government Funding & Startup Schemes (FOCUS)

## What is a Startup?

- New business with:
    
    - **Innovation** and scalability.
        
    - High risk & growth potential.
        
- Indian policy definition (simplified):
    
    - Up to **10 years** old.
        
    - Turnover ≤ **₹100 crore**.
        
    - Working on innovation/product/improvement.
        

## Startup India – Key Points

- **Launched**: **2016** (by DPIIT).
    
- Objectives:
    
    - Promote innovation and entrepreneurship.
        
    - Create jobs.
        
    - Develop startup ecosystem.
        

**Major Benefits**

- **Fund of Funds**:
    
    - Size: **₹10,000 crore**.
        
    - Managed by **SIDBI**.
        
- **Tax**:
    
    - Income tax exemption for **3 years** (within first **10 years**).
        
- **IP / Patents**:
    
    - **80% rebate** on patent filing fees.
        
    - **Fast‑track** patent examination.
        
- **Compliance**:
    
    - Self‑certification for certain labour laws.
        
    - Easier public procurement rules (no past experience/turnover required).
        
- **Support**:
    
    - Startup India Hub, incubators, mentorship, events.
        

## 10,000 Startups – NASSCOM Initiative

- Run by **NASSCOM**.
    
- Aim: support/scale **10,000 tech startups**.
    
- Focus: IT/software products, digital, AI, etc.
    
- Offers mentoring, incubators, investor connects.
    

## STP & SEZ (Export Promotion – Software)

**STP (Software Technology Parks)**

- Provided via **STPI** (Software Technology Parks of India).
    
- Benefits:
    
    - Infrastructure, high‑speed data.
        
    - Duty‑free imports for export units.
        
    - Simplified export procedures.
        

**SEZ (Special Economic Zone)**

- Special area with:
    
    - Tax benefits, duty‑free imports.
        
    - Simplified regulations.
        
- IT companies can set up units to export services/software.
    

## Laws for Startups

- Formation: **Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership, OPC**.
    
- Cyber & data: **IT Act 2000**, other data protection laws.
    
- IP laws: Patents Act, Trademarks Act, Copyright.
    

## US Schemes in Syllabus (Know Names Only)

- **SBIR** – small business innovation research (US).
    
- **STTR** – small business + research institution (US).
    
- **NSF grants** – US science funding.

## Unit IV – Startup in IT (Business Plan) – FOCUS

## Business Plan – Sections (as per syllabus)

1. **Executive Summary**
    
    - Short overview (idea, market, ask).
        
2. **General Company Description**
    
    - Legal form, location, founders, mission, vision, objectives.
        
3. **Products & Services**
    
    - What you’re offering, USP, tech stack.
        
4. **Marketing Plan**
    
    - Target customers, market size, competitors.
        
    - Pricing, promotion, distribution.
        
5. **Operational Plan**
    
    - Daily operations: infrastructure, tools, processes, staffing.
        
6. **Management & Organization**
    
    - Org chart: CEO, CTO, developers, sales, etc.
        
    - Roles, responsibilities.
        
7. **Personal Financial Statement**
    
    - Founder’s assets, liabilities, net worth, investment capacity.
        
8. **Startup Expenses & Capitalization**
    
    - Initial costs: office, hardware, salaries, marketing, legal, IP.
        
    - How funded: equity, loans, grants, own money.
        
9. **Financial Plan**
    
    - Revenue model (subscription, license, etc.).
        
    - 3–5 year projections, break‑even, cash flow.
        
10. **Appendices**
    
    - Resumes, detailed research, IP docs, letters of intent.
        
11. **Refining the Plan**
    
    - Iterative improvements based on feedback/data.
        

## Product‑Based vs Services‑Based Startup (IT)

- Product‑based:
    
    - Own software product (e.g., SaaS).
        
    - High R&D, global scale.
        
- Services‑based:
    
    - Custom development, consulting, support.
        
    - Project and contract work.
        

**Examples**

- Product: Zoho, Freshworks.
    
- Service: TCS, Infosys, Wipro.

## Unit V – Companies: IT & ITES, NASSCOM, STPI, Trends, Diversity (HIGH FOCUS)

## IT vs ITES

- **IT** – building technology (software, hardware, networks).
    
- **ITES** – services delivered using IT (BPO, call centers, online support, etc.).
    

## Product‑Based vs Services‑Based (Table)

|Feature|Product‑Based|Services‑Based|
|---|---|---|
|Main offering|Own products (e.g., SaaS, apps)|IT services & solutions for clients|
|Revenue|Product sales, licenses, subscriptions|Project fees, contracts|
|R&D focus|High – for own roadmap|Moderate – based on client needs|
|Examples|Microsoft, Zoho, Google, Freshworks|TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL|

## NASSCOM (Very Important GK)

- Full form: **National Association of Software and Service Companies**.
    
- **Established**: **1988**.
    
- Type: Non‑profit industry association.
    
- Role:
    
    - Apex body for India’s IT‑BPM industry.
        
    - Policy advocacy with government.
        
    - Promotes quality, IP, skills, startups (10,000 Startups).
        
- Represents India’s large tech industry (hundreds of billions of dollars).
    

## STPI (Software Technology Parks of India)

- **Established**: **1991** (5 June).
    
- Under: **MeitY**.
    
- Functions:
    
    - Implement **STP** and related schemes.
        
    - Provide plug‑and‑play infrastructure for software exporters.
        
    - Support startups and CoEs in new tech (AI, IoT, blockchain, etc.).
        
- Exports:
    
    - From ~₹52 crore (1991) → to **lakh‑crore** level now.
        

## Latest IT Projects & Trends (Global Impact)

- **AI & ML** – generative AI, health, finance, education, climate.
    
- **Cloud & Edge** – cloud‑native, global platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).
    
- **Cybersecurity** – defending against rising cyberattacks, ransomware.
    
- **Digital public infrastructure** – digital ID, digital payments (UPI), health records.
    
- **Blockchain/Web3** – contracts, identity, supply chain.
    
- **IoT & 5G** – smart cities, smart factories.
    

## Diversity in the Workforce

- Diversity = mix of gender, caste, religion, nationality, age, disability, etc.
    
- Benefits:
    
    - Better ideas, innovation, problem solving.
        
    - Better understanding of diverse customers.
        
    - Stronger reputation, talent attraction.
        
- Practices:
    
    - Anti‑discrimination policies.
        
    - Women in tech programs.
        
    - Accessibility and inclusion.

## Unit VI – Ethical & Professional Issues in Information Security (VERY HIGH FOCUS)

## Law vs Ethics

- **Law**:
    
    - Made by government, mandatory, has punishment.
        
- **Ethics**:
    
    - Moral principles, voluntary, social/professional consequences.
        

## Policy vs Law

|Aspect|Policy (Org)|Law (Govt)|
|---|---|---|
|Maker|Organization|Government|
|Scope|Employees/members|Everyone in jurisdiction|
|Enforced by|Management|Courts, police|
|Punishment|Warning, suspension, firing|Fine, imprisonment, compensation|

## Organizational Liability & Counsel

- Liability if:
    
    - Weak security → data breach.
        
    - Privacy violation.
        
- Concepts:
    
    - **Due care** – reasonable measures to protect data.
        
    - **Due diligence** – continuous monitoring and improvement.
        
- Need legal counsel to:
    
    - Interpret laws, write contracts/policies, manage disputes.
        

## Cyber Crime & Trends

- Types:
    
    - Phishing, ransomware, identity theft, online fraud, sextortion, scams.
        
- Very high in India:
    
    - Huge crores lost to cyber fraud.
        
    - Cyberattacks per organization per week are high.
        
- Government response:
    
    - Increased cybersecurity budget.
        
    - Creating cyber crime portals, e‑FIR.
        

## Need for Cyber Law in India

- Main law: **IT Act, 2000**, amended **2008**.
    
- Covers:
    
    - E‑contracts, digital signatures.
        
    - Cyber crimes.
        
    - Some data protection.
        
    - Jurisdiction and evidence.
        

## Ethical Dilemmas in Project Management

- Conflicts:
    
    - Hiding bugs to meet deadlines.
        
    - Misreporting progress.
        
    - Accepting kickbacks or biased vendor choice.
        
    - Misusing data or access.
        
- Professional duty:
    
    - Honesty, fairness, transparency.
        
    - Protect users and data, not just deadlines.
        

## Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)

|Method|Key Idea|Binding?|
|---|---|---|
|Arbitration|Neutral arbitrator gives final award|Usually **binding**|
|Mediation|Mediator facilitates, no decision|**Non‑binding** unless agreed|
|Conciliation|Similar to mediation; conciliator suggests solutions|Usually non‑binding until settlement|
|Negotiation|Parties talk directly|Depends on agreement|

- ADR = faster, cheaper, more confidential than courts.
    

## Cyber Security Tools (Hands‑On)

- **Antivirus / EDR** – detect and remove malware.
    
- **Firewall** – control network traffic in/out.
    
- **SIEM** – collect & analyze logs, detect incidents.
    
- **Vulnerability scanners** – find weaknesses.
    
- **IDS/IPS** – detect and prevent intrusions.
    
- **Encryption tools** – protect data.
    
- **Pen‑testing tools** – test security (e.g., Metasploit).
