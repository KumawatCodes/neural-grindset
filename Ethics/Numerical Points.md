## Unit I – Ethics: Time‑Line / Duration‑Based Points

Unit I is mostly conceptual, but there are some time‑related / exam‑style facts you should know:

## 1. Corporate Ethics & Work Environment (Time‑Related Points)

- **Ethics training**:
    
    - Usually **regular/annual** for employees.
        
    - Many companies do it **once a year** or at least **once every 2 years**.
        
    - For exam: “Ethics training should be **regular**, typically **annual**.”
        
- **Code of conduct**:
    
    - Should be **in writing** and **updated regularly** (e.g., **every 1–3 years** or when laws change).
        
    - For exam: “Code of ethics should be **reviewed and updated periodically**, often **every 1–3 years**.”
        
- **Ethics audits**:
    
    - Many organizations do ethics/compliance audits **annually** or **every 2 years**.
        
    - For exam: “Ethics audits are usually done **annually** or **periodically**.”
        
- **Whistle‑blower protection**:
    
    - Policies should allow employees to report concerns **at any time** (no time limit).
        

These are not “fixed by law” in India, but they are **common practice** and often appear in MCQs as “how often should ethics training be done?” → **regularly / annually**.

## Unit II – Intellectual Property: All Time‑Line & Duration Points (Very Important)

This is the **most important unit for timeline/duration questions**.  
You must remember:

## 1. Patent

- **Term (duration)**:
    
    - **20 years** from the **date of filing** the patent application.[[en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_of_patent)]
        
    - Not from the date of grant.
        
- **Started from**:
    
    - **Filing date** of the application.
        
- **Renewal / Extension**:
    
    - **Cannot be extended** beyond 20 years.[[babariaip](https://babariaip.com/blog/when-and-how-to-renew-your-patents-trademarks-and-copyrights/)]
        
    - Must pay **annual maintenance fees** to keep it in force.
        
    - If fees are not paid → **patent lapses** before 20 years.
        
- **After 20 years**:
    
    - Invention becomes **public domain** – anyone can use it without permission.
        

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “A patent in India is valid for _ years from the filing date.” → **20 years**.
    
- “Can a patent be renewed after 20 years?” → **No**.
    
- “From which date does the patent term start?” → **Filing date**.
    
- “What happens if annual fees are not paid?” → **Patent lapses**.
    

---

## 2. Copyright

- **For most works** (literary, artistic, musical, dramatic, software code):
    
    - Duration = **Life of the author + 60 years** after the end of the year in which the author dies.[[babariaip](https://babariaip.com/blog/when-and-how-to-renew-your-patents-trademarks-and-copyrights/)]
        
- **For anonymous/pseudonymous works, cinematograph films, sound recordings, government works**:
    
    - Duration = **60 years** from the end of the year of **publication**.
        
- **Renewal**:
    
    - **No renewal** needed. Protection is **automatic** and lasts for the full term.[[babariaip](https://babariaip.com/blog/when-and-how-to-renew-your-patents-trademarks-and-copyrights/)]
        
- **Started from**:
    
    - For life+60: author’s death (end of that year).
        
    - For 60‑year works: year of publication.
        

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “Copyright in India for most works lasts for _ years after the author’s death.” → **60 years**.
    
- “Does copyright need to be renewed every few years?” → **No**.
    
- “For a film, copyright lasts for _ years from publication.” → **60 years**.
    

---

## 3. Trademark

- **Initial registration period**:
    
    - **10 years** from the **date of registration**.[[babariaip](https://babariaip.com/blog/when-and-how-to-renew-your-patents-trademarks-and-copyrights/)]
        
- **Renewal**:
    
    - Can be **renewed indefinitely** for successive **10‑year** periods.
        
    - Each renewal is for **10 years**.
        
    - **No limit** on number of renewals.
        
- **If not renewed**:
    
    - Trademark can be **removed from the register** after a grace period.
        

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “A trademark is initially registered for _ years.” → **10 years**.
    
- “How many times can a trademark be renewed?” → **Indefinitely (unlimited)**.
    
- “Each renewal of a trademark is for _ years.” → **10 years**.
    
- “Can a trademark be protected forever?” → **Yes**, if renewed every 10 years.
    

---

## 4. Industrial Design (if mentioned in syllabus)

- **Initial term**: **10 years** from the date of registration.
    
- **Extension**: Can be extended by **5 years** (total **15 years**).
    
- **Beyond 15 years**: **Not renewable**.

## Unit III – Government Funding & Startup Schemes: All Time‑Line / Year‑Based Points

This unit has many **year‑based GK questions** (when was it launched, how many years ago, etc.).

## 1. Start-up India

- **Launched**: **15 February 2016**.
    
- **Under**: **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)**, Ministry of Commerce & Industry.
    
- **Current year**: 2026 →
    
    - Start‑up India has been running for:
        
        - 2026 − 2016 = **10 years** (as of 2026).
            

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “Start‑up India was launched in which year?” → **2016**.
    
- “How many years has Start‑up India been running as of 2026?” → **10 years**.
    
- “Which department is nodal for Start‑up India?” → **DPIIT**.
    

---

## 2. Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS)

- **Amount**: **₹10,000 crore**.
    
- **Managed by**: **SIDBI** (Small Industries Development Bank of India).
    
- **Purpose**: Invest in startup‑focused venture funds.
    
- This is a **fixed amount**; in MCQs they may ask:
    
    - “Fund of Funds for Startups has crore.” → **₹10,000 crore**.
        
    - “Managed by _?” → **SIDBISIDBI**.
        

---

## 3. Tax Benefits for Startups

- **Corporate tax exemption**:
    
    - **3 consecutive years** of tax exemption for eligible startups (subject to conditions).
        
- **Start date**:
    
    - Any **3 consecutive years** within **10 years** from incorporation.
        

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “Eligible startups get tax exemption for _ years.” → **3 years**.
    
- “Within how many years from incorporation can they take 3 years of tax exemption?” → **Within 10 years**.
    

---

## 4. Patent Fee Rebate for Startups (Start‑up India)

- **Rebate on patent filing fees**: **80%**.
    
- **Fast‑track examination** of patent applications for startups.
    

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “Startups get _% rebate on patent filing fees under Start‑up India.” → **80%80%**.
    

---

## 5. 10,000 Startups – NASSCOM Initiative

- **Initiative name**: **“10,000 Startups – A NASSCOM Initiative”**.
    
- **Focus**: Tech startups in **IT sector**.
    
- **NASSCOM**:
    
    - **Established**: **1 March 1988**.
        
    - By **2026**: 2026 − 1988 = **38 years**.
        

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “10,000 Startups is an initiative of which organization?” → **NASSCOM**.
    
- “NASSCOM was established in which year?” → **1988**.
    
- “How many years had NASSCOM been working by 2026?” → **38 years**.

## 6. STPI (Software Technology Parks of India)

- **Established**: **1991** (specifically **5 June 1991**).[[indiascienceandtechnology.gov](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/organisations/ministry-and-departments/ministry-electronics-and-information-technology-meity-govt-india/software-technology-parks-india-stpi-uttar-pradesh)][[youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IogRLpp7ks)]
    
- **Under Ministry**: **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**.
    
- By **2026**: 2026 − 1991 = **35 years**.
    
- Started from **3 centers** in 1990s → now **63 centers** (as of 2023–2024).
    
- Software exports:
    
    - 1991: **₹52 crore**
        
    - 2021–22: **₹5 lakh crore**
        
    - 2023: **over ₹7 lakh crore**.
        

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “STPI was established in which year?” → **1991**.
    
- “How many years had STPI been operating by 2026?” → **35 years**.
    
- “STPI is under which ministry?” → **MeitY**.
    
- “STPI registration helped exports grow from ₹52 crore in 1991 to over _ in 2023.” → **₹7 lakh crore**.

- **First SEZs**: Operational from **2006**.
    
- **Purpose**: Promote exports, investment, and infrastructure.
    

**Exam‑style facts**:

- “SEZ Act was passed in which year?” → **2005**.
    
- “First SEZs became operational from which year?” → **2006**.

## 8. Bank Loans for Startups (Mudra, CGTMSE, etc.)

These don’t have fixed “year” in syllabus, but important duration‑type points:

- **Mudra Yojana**:
    
    - For micro‑enterprises and women entrepreneurs.
        
    - Loans are generally **short to medium term** (e.g., 1–5 years).
        
- **CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises)**:
    
    - Provides **collateral‑free loans** to small businesses/startups.
        
    - Loan period varies, but often **up to 5–10 years** depending on scheme.
        

For exam:

- “Which scheme provides collateral‑free loans to small businesses?” → **CGTMSE**.
    
- “Mudra Yojana mainly supports _?” → **women and micro‑entrepreneurs**.
    

---

## 9. SBIR, STTR, NSF Grants (US‑based)

- These are **US government** schemes, not Indian.
    
- No fixed “year of establishment” needed for typical Indian exam.
    
- Key point:
    
    - **SBIR**: funds small businesses for R&D.
        
    - **STTR**: collaboration between small business and research institution.
        
    - **NSF**: grants for scientific research.
